PARAMPARAGAT KRISHI VIKAS YOJANA (PKVY) — ORGANIC FARMING SUPPORT

What is it?
PKVY promotes organic farming through a cluster-based approach. Groups of farmers in the same area adopt organic farming methods together, and the government provides financial assistance to cover the transition costs, certification, and marketing support.

Who is eligible?
- Any farmer — owner, tenant, or sharecropper — willing to adopt organic farming practices
- Farmers must join or form a cluster of at least 50 farmers, covering at least 50 acres of contiguous or nearby land
- An individual farmer generally cannot apply alone; enrollment happens through the cluster

What is the benefit?
₹50,000 per hectare is provided over a 3-year period, covering:
- Organic inputs (compost, bio-fertilizers, bio-pesticides)
- Certification costs (organic certification is required to sell produce as "certified organic")
- Value addition, packaging, and marketing support for organic produce

Why does this matter for farmers?
Organic certification allows farmers to sell their produce at premium prices in dedicated organic markets. The cluster approach also means farmers share knowledge, inputs, and market access, rather than navigating the transition to organic farming alone.

How to apply
Through the local agriculture department or Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK), which typically helps identify or form farmer clusters in a region. Interested farmers can express interest to their local agriculture officer to be included in an upcoming cluster formation.

Required documents
- 7/12 utara (land record)
- Aadhaar card
- Bank account
- Cluster group registration/membership proof (obtained once a cluster is formed)

Note: Cluster formation timelines and available clusters vary by district — confirm current cluster availability with your local agriculture office before assuming eligibility.
