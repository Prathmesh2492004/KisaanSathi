MODIFIED INTEREST SUBVENTION SCHEME (MISS)

What is it?
MISS makes short-term crop loans cheaper for farmers by reducing the effective interest rate they pay. Farmers who take a crop loan through their Kisan Credit Card (KCC) and repay it promptly can access loans up to ₹3 lakh at an effective interest rate of just 4% per year — much lower than standard loan rates.

Who is eligible?
Any farmer who already holds a Kisan Credit Card (KCC) and takes a short-term crop loan through it. There is no separate application — this benefit is applied automatically by the lending bank on qualifying loans.

How does the interest rate work?
- The base lending rate for crop loans up to ₹3 lakh is typically 7% per year
- The government provides a 2% interest subvention (discount), bringing the effective rate down to 5%
- If the farmer repays the loan promptly (on or before the due date), an additional 3% Prompt Repayment Incentive is applied, bringing the effective rate down further to 4%

Why does this matter?
This significantly lowers the cost of borrowing for crop production, post-harvest expenses, and maintenance of farm equipment — as long as the farmer repays on time. Late repayment forfeits the prompt repayment discount, so the effective rate rises back toward the base rate.

Required documents
- Existing Kisan Credit Card
- Loan account details from the issuing bank
- Aadhaar card

How to apply
No separate application needed — this is automatically applied by the bank to eligible KCC crop loans up to ₹3 lakh. Farmers should confirm with their bank that their loan is registered as an eligible short-term crop loan to receive the subvention.

Note: Base interest rates, subvention percentages, and loan limits are set by the government and Reserve Bank of India and can be revised — confirm current rates with your bank or the official agriculture ministry portal.
