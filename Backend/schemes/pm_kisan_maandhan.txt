PM KISAN MAANDHAN YOJANA — FARMER PENSION SCHEME

What is it?
PM Kisan Maandhan Yojana is a voluntary, contributory pension scheme for small and marginal farmers. It guarantees a fixed monthly pension of ₹3,000 after the farmer turns 60 years old, helping provide financial security in old age when farming income typically declines.

Who is eligible?
- Small and marginal farmers with cultivable land holding up to 2 hectares (as recorded in the 7/12 utara or equivalent land record)
- Age between 18 and 40 years at the time of enrollment
- Must NOT already be covered under another government pension or social security scheme (such as EPFO, ESIC, or NPS)
- Must NOT be an income tax payer
- Family members holding government posts above Group D, or registered professionals (doctors, engineers, lawyers, chartered accountants), are excluded

How does it work?
The farmer contributes a small monthly amount ranging from ₹55 to ₹200, depending on their age at entry (the younger the entry age, the lower the monthly contribution). The government contributes an equal matching amount to the pension fund. Contributions continue until the farmer reaches 60 years of age, after which the guaranteed pension of ₹3,000/month begins.

What if the farmer already receives PM-KISAN?
Farmers already enrolled in PM-KISAN can have their contribution auto-debited from their PM-KISAN benefit amount if they choose, making enrollment simpler.

What happens if the farmer dies before age 60?
The spouse can continue the scheme by paying the remaining contributions, or can exit and receive the farmer's contribution portion along with accumulated interest.

Required documents
- 7/12 utara (land record)
- Aadhaar card
- Bank passbook with IFSC code
- Age proof document

Where to apply
Common Service Centres (CSC), or online through the official PM Kisan Maandhan Yojana portal, or through the same channels used for PM-KISAN enrollment.

Note: Contribution amounts, age brackets, and eligibility conditions should be verified against the current official scheme guidelines, as these are periodically revised.
