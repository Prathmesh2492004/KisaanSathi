PUNYASHLOK AHILYADEVI HOLKAR KARJMAFI YOJANA — MAHARASHTRA LOAN WAIVER (2026)

What is it?
This is a farm loan waiver scheme announced by the Maharashtra state government in its 2026 budget, named after Ahilyadevi Holkar. It waives overdue short-term crop loans for eligible farmers, aiming to reduce debt burden caused by crop losses, rising input costs, and unpredictable weather.

Who is eligible?
- Farmers residing in Maharashtra
- Must have taken a short-term crop loan from a nationalised bank, district cooperative bank, or Regional Rural Bank (RRB)
- The loan must have been overdue as of 30 September 2025
- Small and marginal farmers are the priority beneficiaries

What does it cover?
- Waiver of up to ₹2,00,000 per eligible borrower on overdue short-term crop loans
- An additional ₹50,000 incentive is offered to farmers who repaid their loans regularly during the specified period (rewarding good repayment behavior, not just waiving debt)

How does it work?
Once the official portal is notified and operational, the waiver amount is expected to be credited directly to the farmer's loan account through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) — no separate cash disbursal to the farmer's personal account.

Required documents
- 7/12 utara (land record)
- Aadhaar card
- Bank loan account details, including outstanding balance statement
- Loan sanction letter (if available)

Where to apply
Through the official state portal once notified (expected to follow the same pattern as the earlier Mahatma Jyotiba Phule Shetkari Karjmukti Yojana, at a dedicated maharashtra.gov.in subdomain), or through your bank branch / district cooperative bank.

Important note
This scheme was newly announced in the 2026 state budget. As of the time this document was written, the official application portal and exact disbursal timeline were still being finalized. Confirm current application status with your bank or local agriculture office before relying on this information — details are likely to be updated as the scheme rolls out.
