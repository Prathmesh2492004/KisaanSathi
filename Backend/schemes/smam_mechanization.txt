SUB-MISSION ON AGRICULTURAL MECHANIZATION (SMAM)

What is it?
SMAM provides subsidies to farmers for purchasing farm machinery and equipment — tractors, power tillers, threshers, harvesters, seed drills, and other tools that reduce manual labor and increase efficiency. The scheme also supports Custom Hiring Centres (CHCs), where farmers who cannot afford their own equipment can rent machinery at subsidized rates.

Who is eligible?
- Any farmer — owner, tenant, or sharecropper — planning to purchase eligible farm equipment
- No minimum or maximum land holding requirement
- Priority given to small and marginal farmers, and to farmers in the North Eastern states

Subsidy amount
- General category farmers: 40-50% subsidy on equipment cost
- SC/ST, women, and small/marginal farmers: higher subsidy, often up to 50-80% depending on the equipment and state top-up
- Custom Hiring Centres: separate, larger subsidy for setting up equipment rental businesses that serve multiple farmers

How to apply
1. Register on the state's DBT agriculture portal (MahaDBT for Maharashtra)
2. Select the equipment category you want to purchase
3. Get a quotation from an empaneled (government-approved) dealer
4. Submit the application with land record, Aadhaar, and bank details
5. Once approved, purchase the equipment and submit proof of purchase to claim the subsidy

Required documents
- 7/12 utara (land record)
- Aadhaar card
- Bank account details
- Quotation from an empaneled dealer

Important notes
- There are often annual caps on how many subsidy approvals are issued per district or village — apply early in the financial year
- Equipment must be purchased from an empaneled dealer for the subsidy to be valid
- A farmer typically cannot claim subsidy on the same equipment category more than once within a set number of years

Note: Subsidy percentages and equipment eligibility lists vary by state and are revised periodically — confirm current rates on your state's DBT portal before applying.
